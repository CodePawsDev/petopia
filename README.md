# 🐾 PETOPIA

> Everything your pet needs, all in one place.

PETOPIA is a modern full-stack platform that helps Pet Parents manage every aspect of their pets' lives—from booking services and tracking medical records to shopping, memberships, and more—all in one place.

---

## ✨ Tech Stack

- Next.js 16
- React 19
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS v4
- shadcn/ui
- Supabase
- PostgreSQL
- Vercel

---

## 🚀 Core Features

### Pet Parent

- Pet profile management
- Multi-pet support
- Service booking
- View medical records
- Upload pet health documents
- Pet health timeline
- Membership & reward points
- Product orders

### Business Dashboard

- Dashboard & analytics
- Service management
- Product management
- Booking management
- Pet Parent management
- Medical record management
- Promotions & coupons
- Team management

---

## ❤️ Product Vision

PETOPIA follows a **Pet-first philosophy**.

Every feature in the platform revolves around each pet's profile, health, and lifecycle—not just bookings or products.

The platform is designed to deliver a friendly, modern, and seamless experience for both Pet Parents and pet care businesses.

---

## 🏗️ Architecture Principles

- Pet-first domain model
- Feature-based architecture
- Mobile-first responsive design
- Multi-language ready
- Multi-business ready (future)
- Type-safe development with TypeScript
